**Mcfly live fetch:** 2026-09-09  
**Title:** “Mcfly Analytics - Ad spend next to store sales — Total ROAS +...”  
**Price:** $39/month · 7-day trial  
**Reviews:** 0.0 (0)  
**Launched:** September 7, 2026  
**Hero (full):** “See ad spend next to store sales — including billboards. Total ROAS = sales ÷ spend you added. Store Analytics tracks sales, not the ad spend you actually paid. Mcfly is the cash desk that puts Meta, Google, TikTok, email, and billboard cost next to your store sales for the same days. Add spend by day or CSV. ## Line-by-line Mcfly listing critique

1. **Title truncation** “Total ROAS +…” — Shopify cuts it. Put the formula or “$39 cash desk” in the first 40 characters.
2. **“Store Analytics tracks sales, not the ad spend”** — true (Community 134251). Strong. Keep.
3. **“Paid plan adds LTV… and Goals”** after stating $39 as *the* plan — implies a free vs paid that **does not exist** on the price card. Confusing. Either ship a free plan or delete “Paid plan adds.”
4. **“mailto” in bullet 5** — looks like leftover. Unprofessional.
5. **“no pixels / MTA” in the same bullet as LTV** — jammed. Also feeds the pixel graph.
6. **7-day trial on a paste product** — mismatch (see APP_STORE_MARKET).
7. **0 reviews / 0 rating** — the first thing a merchant sees after price.
8. **Data access:** customers + **all order history** + device/activity. For a spend-vs-sales desk, “all order history” needs the LTV claim to be true; otherwise it looks greedy vs the job.
9. **Developer address** — virtual-suite pattern. Not fatal. Changelog + support SLA would help more.
10. **Screenshots +2** — too few vs Polar/TrueProfit.
