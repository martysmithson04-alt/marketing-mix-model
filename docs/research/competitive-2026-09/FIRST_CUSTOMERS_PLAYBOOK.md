### 2.1 Triple Whale — own brand → Twitter DMs → influencers → agencies

**Plays (FOUNDER_RETELL — not audited by us):**

| Play | What they did | Mcfly adapt | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Internal tool first** | Built Summary Page for Madison Braids / their own 8-figure brands; hours of Sheets were the pain | Run Mcfly on a **real** store you operate, every Monday, in public | If you don’t operate a store, AdsX says it shows in outreach. Empathy is visible. |
| **Cold DM operators** | AJ Orbach DMed DTC owner-operators on Twitter; no ad spend; no formal launch | 20 personal emails/DMs a week to operators who **show Meta + Shopify** (Script A). Ask for a reply, not an install | 2026 Twitter ≠ 2021 DTC Twitter. Slack/Community/Reddit may be the room now. |
| **Influencer try + rave** | A few big operators used it and raved; Shaan Puri tweet as amplification | Do **not** hunt “influencers” at 0. Hunt **one** operator who will put the number in Slack. Amplification without a ritual is vapor | Paying for shoutouts that look like reviews = policy-adjacent |
| **Community machine** | Slack of thousands; live events; “What do you think?”; ship unfinished | A **12-person** design-partner Slack is enough. Daily “what did Monday’s number change?” | Empty Slack is worse than none |
| **Agency channel** | Maxx: agencies were a **primary** sales focus; later ~**1/3 of revenue** `VENDOR/FOUNDER` | Script B. One agency with 8 clients > 40 cold founders. Polar $750 GMV-tax is your opening | $39 × N with no agency SKU — they will pick Kleio $29 unlimited |

**Four-score steal:** founder-market fit + DM hustle + agency batching + feedback speed. Not the suite.

## 3. Policy-legal review engine (copy this, do not get clever)

From Shopify official + BigMoves + TSC 2026:

| Allowed | Forbidden |
| --- | --- |
| Neutral “how’s your experience — you can review here” after **successful workflow** | “Leave a 5-star” |
| Reviews API / official modal | Discount, extra month, feature unlock, gift card for a review |
| Ask **all** eligible paid/active users | Ask only people you think are happy (sentiment filter) |
| Ask after first **computed week** | Ask at install, or during trial if they are ineligible |
| Reply to 1★ with fix + limitation | Argue, bribe in public, “email us for refund if you delete the review” |
| Deep link to WriteReviewModal | Testimonials on listing images |

**Eligibility reminder:** trial-only merchants may **not** be able to review (BigMoves cites Dec 2024 change — `MARKET_REPORT`; confirm in Partner docs before you build a trial-ask). Prefer **paid week-1**.

**Mcfly TTV problem:** 7-day trial + CSV means many never reach “successful workflow.” That is why R4-B (14-day) and R3 (auto-spend) sit under every review plan. Asking earlier creates 1-stars (“empty app”).

## 5. Design-partner contract (what to offer / what not to)

**Offer (legal, useful):**
- Founder Slack + one setup call.  
- You paste **their** last 14 days with them.  
- Written definitions: sales field, tax, refund clock, spend source.  
- Next Monday: you send the number; they tell you if they **changed spend**.  
- Extended trial **not** tied to a review (if you extend, extend **everyone** in the cohort, not reviewers).  
- Roadmap vote.

**Do not offer:**
- Free-for-5-stars.  
- “Design partner pricing” that is actually a review bribe.  
- Equity.  
- “We’ll feature you on the listing” (forbidden).  
- You may feature them on **mcflyads.com** with permission — that does not help the listing.

**Kill criterion (already in MASTER_PLAN / SYNTHESIS):** partner will not open the weekly number after 30 days. Uninstall. Do not nag a review out of a corpse.

**Success criterion:** they put the number in a **client Slack or board appendix** (Script commitment rung 5). That sentence is worth more than a star.

## 7. First-value instrumentation (you cannot ask without this)

AdsX / Shopify: ask at the **end of a successful workflow**.

Define success in code/docs (research, not a ship order):

1. Shop has **orders** in range.  
2. Shop has **spend > 0** in range (pasted or synced).  
3. Total ROAS **computed**.  
4. User viewed break-even or exported / copied the number.  
5. Optional: they returned on a second session ≥6 days later (ritual).

Then: in-app neutral ask **or** founder email with the official modal link.  
If step 2 never happens (empty paste box), **do not ask**. That is the 7-day / paste death spiral.

## 12. Research call (not a ship order)

The winners’ first-10 is always the same physics:

1. **They were the customer** (TW brands, Kleio operator, Polar growth/data founders).  
2. **They talked daily** (DMs, Slack, 2-hour calls, named CS).  
3. **They asked at first value, never for stars.**  
4. **They batched via agencies** once the ritual worked.  
5. **They did not wait for the store.**

Mcfly has (1) as a hypothesis and (2)–(5) as a vacuum.

**If the founder will do 20 personal conversations in 14 days,** the rest of WAVE D is usable.  
**If not,** Kleio keeps the religion, TrueProfit keeps the reviews, Sheets keep the job, and the listing stays a tombstone.
