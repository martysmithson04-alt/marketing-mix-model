“always missing something” — Community 588628

### Win-frame (use if…)
**“The Sheet is the draft. Mcfly is the lock.”**

Use when:
- They already compute sales ÷ spend and the pain is **staleness, error, or two humans arguing about the cell**.
- They have **offline / retainer / billboard** lines that never stay updated.
- An agency needs a **client-safe** number that is not a 40-tab workbook.

Copy that is fair today:
- Formula on the tin: sales ÷ spend you added.
- Break-even from a stated margin.
- Offline channels the Sheet always forgets.
- Same math every Monday (no 30-day lookback rewrite — Community 180915).

### Lose-frame (use if…)
**“If your Sheet already has COGS, fees, Amazon, and a model you trust, we are a $39 cell.”**

Do not pick a fight with a controller’s workbook. SyncWith is $4.99. Better Reports will email the report. You have no scheduled artifact (`CURRENT_RELIGION` miss vs PROBLEM 5).

### Referee
The **analyst who owns the tab**, not the founder who hates Excel. If that person loves the model, you lose.

### Religion
`CURRENT_RELIGION` actually fits P3 agencies (PERSONAS). Ease is **medium**, not high — they must re-paste. `RESEARCH_OPTION`: ingest the Sheet / Monday email so you are not asking them to abandon the OS.

### Score (research judgment)
Money M · Love M · Ease **L vs Sheet** · Real problem **real but DIY**. This war is winnable only as **ritual productization**, which Better Reports already proved with 1,199 reviews.

## 2. vs free Shopify Analytics (the enemy every listing must name)

### Battlefield
Admin → Analytics → Reports → Marketing. “Sales attributed to marketing.” Shop Campaigns. Profit by product. Sidekick answers.

### Their sentence
Shopify Help: sales attributed to marketing = sales from traffic Shopify can tie to campaigns created in Shopify **or** third parties that share data. Cost exists for **Shopify-created** marketing activities. Third-party app reports “include only the information available to Shopify from the app.” https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/analyze-marketing/marketing-performance

Community 134251: “Shopify definitely doesn’t measure the spend. You will need a third-party app.”

Polar 2026 plan table: **no ad-spend ingest on Basic, Grow, Advanced, or Plus**. Attribution **models** unlock on Grow+. https://www.polaranalytics.com/post/shopify-attribution-models-explained-which-one-should-you-use

Bybtraction 2026 (practitioner): same “no ad spend integration” across plans — `MARKET_REPORT` https://bybtraction.com/shopify-plan-differences-analytics-reports-attribution/

Community 180915: attributed sales **move** after close (30-day lookback).

Price Geek: “Does Shopify Analytics show profit? No.”

### Win-frame
**“Admin has sales. It does not have your Meta invoice. It does not have the billboard. And last quarter’s attributed number will twitch.”**

This is the **cleanest win Mcfly has**. It is also **insufficient** as a product (PROBLEM_BANK #3: commercially under-owned; people DIY or buy profit/suites).

Specific, legal claims:
- Spend ingest: not Meta/Google/TikTok/billboard. `EVIDENCE` Community + Polar + Help Center.
- Lookback mutation: `EVIDENCE` Community 180915.
- Profit: Cost per item ≠ ads+fees+labels. `EVIDENCE` Community 657805.
- Plan gating: don’t tell a Plus merchant they have “no marketing reports.” They have models. They still lack spend.

### Lose-frame
**“If you only run Shop Campaigns, ShopifyQL already has spend + ROAS.”**  
`shop_campaign_insights` — official. Do not lie.

**“If you want last-click channel credit, Admin already does that on Grow+.”**  
Buying Mcfly for UTM last-click is a category error.

**“If you want heatmaps, install Clarity Free.”**  
Shopify already thinks that’s what you are.

### Referee
Sidekick + the listing’s first 50 words. If those words say “analytics / insights / visuals,” you stay in Clarity’s aisle.

### Kleio note
Kleio has the same rail problem **with better copy** (“P&L,” “ad integrations”). Rail is not destiny, but 0 reviews + pixel neighbors is a bounce.

### Score
Money L–M (the hole is real; WTP leaks to TP/TW/Sheets) · Love L until auto-spend · Ease L (paste vs Admin already open) · Real problem **H**.
