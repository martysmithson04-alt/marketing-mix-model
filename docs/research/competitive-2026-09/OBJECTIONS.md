## 0. The meta-objection (silence death)

**They will not say this out loud. They will bounce.**

> “0.0 (0 reviews). Launched September 7. Adjacent to free pixels.”

`EVIDENCE`: https://apps.shopify.com/mcfly-analytics-public · Shopify merchandises Clarity / WeTracked / Parkour on the same rail.

**What they do:** close the tab. TSC MARKET_REPORT: <25 reviews → ~1–2% view-to-install (https://taylorsicard.com/blog/shopify-app-listing-conversion). Directionally correct even if the percents are gossip.

**You cannot handle this on the listing.** Shopify forbids testimonials in listing copy/images (req 4.3.7). The only legal social proof **is** reviews. At zero, the listing is a hostage note.

**Sales-call handling (outbound only):**

| Do | Don’t |
| --- | --- |
| “We’re two days of public listing old. I’m the human. I’ll compute your last week with you. If it’s useless, uninstall same day.” | “We’re the next Triple Whale.” |
| Name the formula. Do the math on **their** numbers. | Point at SAMPLE Harbor Home Co as proof |
| Offer a walkthrough, not a brand | Promise “hundreds of brands” |

**Lose-honestly:** if they require a Magic summary (≥100 written + 4.0) or a Named Enterprise CSM before they will install, you lose. Polar/Elevar bought **a person**. You do not have Juan, Bryce, Nora, or Mathias-in-the-wild yet.

`SILENCE_DEATH`: every week at 0 reviews makes this objection **worse**, not “early.” Kleio launched Feb 19, 2025 and now has 20×5★. That is what “early” looks like after the founder actually talks.

## 1. How to use this bank

On a **listing**, you get one hero + five bullets. You do not rebut 40 objections there. Put the **one** enemy in the first 50 words (LISTING_PATTERN P06).

On a **call**, listen for the category, then pick **one** win-frame. Stacking three frames sounds like a suite. You are not a suite.

If the objection is really “I want net profit / pixel / GL”: do not morph Mcfly mid-sentence. Hand them the winner (TrueProfit, Parkour, A2X) and keep the cash-desk lane. Kleio already occupies “anti-attribution + P&L + $29.” See wars file.
