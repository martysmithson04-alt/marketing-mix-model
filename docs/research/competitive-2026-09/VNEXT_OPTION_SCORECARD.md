**Religion-flex is not “how holy.”** It is “how well we use the permission to bend without becoming Triple Whale.” S6 scores **low** here because inflexibility is how the listing stays at 0. S5 (build a pixel) scores **low** because that bend is a **break**.

Composite = sum (max 25). Ties broken by **feasibility** then **anti-pattern load** (`ANTI_PATTERNS.md`). Weights are equal on purpose — if the founder cares only about money, ignore the sum and read the Money column.
