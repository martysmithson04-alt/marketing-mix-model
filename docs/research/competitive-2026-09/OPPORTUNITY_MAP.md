## H. Ruthless filter (apply before any future ship)

An opportunity ships into consideration only if **all** are true:

1. A public URL shows people already paying or reviewing for it.
2. It improves at least two of {money, love, ease, real problem} without collapsing the other two.
3. We can state the **metric definition** in one sentence a bookkeeper won’t 1-star.
4. It does not require us to win a category that already has a 5.0 / 800+ review owner **unless** we have a wedge they structurally refuse (offline spend, anti-claim, flat price).

**TrueProfit structurally refuses:** billboards/retainers as first-class, anti-path copy, flat-not-order-meter (they surcharge per order).  
**TW structurally refuses:** price honesty, VAT humility, not being an OS.  
**Shopify Admin structurally refuses:** ingesting Meta/Google/billboard spend.

Those three refusals are the only durable wedges visible on 2026-09-09.
