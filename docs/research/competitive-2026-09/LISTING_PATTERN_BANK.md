# Listing pattern bank — what winning App Store pages actually do

**Date:** 2026-09-09  
**Method:** Patterns observed on live listings fetched this run. Each pattern: what it is, who uses it, evidence URL, whether Mcfly does it, religion note.

Shopify forbids testimonials in listing copy/images. Patterns below are **structural**, not fake quote tricks.

---

## P01. Price as a scan line, not a philosophy

**Observed:** Every listing puts price in the header (`$39/month`, `Free`, `From $35/month`, `$750/month`).
**Winners:** Clarity Free; TrueProfit “From $35”; Polar “$750/month” as enterprise filter; Better Reports “From $19.90.”
**Mcfly:** `$39/month. Free trial available.` — clear.
**Miss:** no “from” / no free tier means no “install without thinking.”
**RESEARCH_OPTION:** add a $0 “view-only / 1 channel” plan to change the scan line.

## P02. External-charges disclaimer as a second price

**Observed:** TW, Polar, WeTracked, A2X: “External charges may be billed … separately from your Shopify invoice.”
**Effect:** listing looks cheaper than the real bill (TW GMV slider; WeTracked platform).
**Mcfly:** Shopify-only $39. Honest. Commercially quieter.
**Do not copy** the hidden invoice.

## P03. Tier names that map to a merchant identity

**Observed:**
- TrueProfit: Basic / Advanced / Ultimate / Enterprise
- Lifetimely: FREE / S / M / L + Amazon add-on
- Better Reports: Basic / Grow / Advanced / Plus = **Shopify plan names**
- Elevar: Core / Advanced / Premium
- TW: Free / Foundation / Automate
**Mcfly:** one SKU. No identity ladder.
**RESEARCH_OPTION:** Free / Desk / Agency — not GMV.

## P04. Order-volume meters (category default for profit)

**Observed:** TrueProfit, Lifetimely, BeProfit, Metorik, A2X, Analyzify, Elevar.
**Mcfly CURRENT_RELIGION:** refuse meters (“never a cut of sales”).
**Evidence this is loved:** mixed. TrueProfit 5.0 despite meters. BeProfit 1★ zombie billing. TW 16% 1★ on GMV.
**Keep flat** unless agency seats need another axis.

## P05. Trial length ≥ time-to-value

**Observed:** 14-day is the profit/report default; Metorik 30; A2X 30; Elevar 15; Mcfly **7**.
**Official:** trials configurable; 180-day anti-abuse.
**Pattern:** 7-day is for “2-minute pixel” apps that are actually **free**. Paid desks use 14+.

## P06. First 50 words = job + time-to-value + enemy

**Observed heroes:**
- TrueProfit: “Instantly track your True Profit… Say goodbye to outdated spreadsheets.”
- Parkour: “Setup takes just 2 minutes, no coding required.”
- Polar: “Centralize 45+ data sources… actively grow revenue.”
- TW: “AI operating system… Moby 2.”
- Mcfly: “See ad spend next to store sales — including billboards. Total ROAS = sales ÷ spend you added.”
**Mcfly’s hero is actually good** — specific, formula, offline wedge.
**Weakness:** “Add spend by day or CSV” is the *work*. Winners hide work (“autopilot,” “1-click,” “real-time sync”).

## P07. Five bullets that are features, not sermons

**Observed:** all listings use 5 bullets.
**Mcfly bullets:** formula, BE, CSV channels, Allocation+Goals, LTV + “no pixels/MTA.”
**Last bullet is a refuse.** Winners’ last bullets are **outcomes** (TrueProfit “Mobile app, All-store view”; Polar “Omnichannel reporting”).
**RESEARCH_OPTION:** move “no pixels” to long body; last bullet = Monday email / break-even call.

## P08. “Works with” logo row

**Observed:** TW, Polar, Lifetimely, TrueProfit, Elevar, A2X, Metorik, Clarity.
**Mcfly:** none on fetch.
**Cost of missing:** Sidekick and search have fewer tokens; looks unfinished.
**CURRENT_RELIGION** “no OAuth” **explains** the empty row and **hurts** conversion.
**RESEARCH_OPTION:** even paste-first can list “Works with Shopify Admin · Google Sheets · CSV.” Better than empty.

## P09. Review count as the only legal social proof

**Observed:** Magic summary at ≥100 written + ≥4.0 (Polar, A2X). Better Reports 1,199; TrueProfit 899; Clarity 2,125.
**Mcfly:** 0. The module is an empty wound.
**Cannot** put SAMPLE Harbor Home Co on the listing as a testimonial (req 4.3.7).

## P10. Named human support in visible reviews

**Observed:** Juan, Vani, Durra, Cristian, Sourabh, Raphael, Darshak, Görkem, Bryce, Nora, Juliet.
**Pattern:** enterprise *feel* is a first name.
**Mcfly:** no reviews to hold a name.

## P11. Onboarding-as-a-feature

**Observed:** Elevar / Polar / Analyzify / A2X / Better Reports (“complimentary report service”).
**Listing language:** “15-day trial” + reviews that name the onboarder in the first 11 days.
**Mcfly:** DIY paste. Fast for us, slow for them.

## P12. Popular with stores like yours / Based in United States

**Observed:** Shopify injects this on many listings (TW, Polar, Lifetimely, TrueProfit, SyncWith, Parkour, WeTracked, Elevar, A2X).
**Mcfly:** not shown on fetch (too new / no affinity).
**Not controllable** except by getting installs in the right cohort.

## P13. Languages as a trust badge

**Observed:** Polar EN/FR/DE; TrueProfit EN/FR/ES; Clarity 14 locales.
**Mcfly:** English.
**P4 VAT 1-stars** are EU. Language + tax toggle are the same problem.

## P14. Feature taxonomy checkboxes (Shopify-owned)

**Observed:** long “Show features” trees: Customer behavior, Marketing and sales, Visuals and reports, sometimes Accounting / Ads.
**Mcfly:** Marketing and sales + Visuals and reports — **no Accounting, no Pixel tracking checkbox** (consistent with religion).
**Effect:** search for “profit” / “COGS” / “pixel” will miss Mcfly.
**RESEARCH_OPTION:** if profit-lite ships, tick Accounting. Don’t tick Pixel unless you ship one.

## P15. Screenshot gallery depth

**Observed:** Polar “+6 more”; TrueProfit “+8 more”; Mcfly “+2 more” (fetch).
**Pattern:** suites show 7–9; utilities 3–4.
**Need:** Overview with a **filled** desk (SAMPLE labeled), spend CSV, BE gauge, allocation — not empty states.

## P16. “Featured in” Shopify guides

**Observed:** TW “avoid growing pains”; TrueProfit BFCM + pre-order; Elevar “web pixel apps”; A2X “Sell B2B”; Better Reports scale/service guides.
**Mcfly:** none. Eligibility likely needs reviews/installs.

## P17. Developer gravitas

**Observed:** Microsoft Way; 2017–2021 birth years; firehose of reviews.
**Mcfly:** Sept 7, 2026; West Jordan suite address (shared mailbox pattern — not a judgment, a **trust scan**).
**Cannot fake.** Can add changelog, privacy specificity, SOC later.

## P18. AI in the title

**Observed:** “Polar: AI‑Analytics Platform”; “Microsoft Clarity: AI Insights”; Lifetimely “AI Profit Agent”; TW Moby in hero.
**Mcfly:** no AI. Sidekick may under-recommend.
**RESEARCH_OPTION:** do not rename to “AI.” Do ship “explain this number” later without metering.

## P19. Formula in public

**Observed:** Almost nobody puts `sales ÷ spend` on the listing. Mcfly does.
**This is a genuine differentiator.** Polar mentions MER as a word, not a formula.
**Keep.** Finance-curious buyers need it. Pixel buyers ignore it.

## P20. Offline / billboard as a surprise noun

**Observed:** Mcfly unique in this fetch. Nobody else leads with billboards.
**Pattern:** surprise noun can win memory and lose search.
**Keep in hero *after* the universal job** (“ad spend next to store sales”) — which Mcfly already does.

## P21. Refuse-as-bullet

**Observed:** Mcfly “no pixels / MTA.” Unique.
**Risk:** teaches the graph you are about pixels (negation still tokens “pixel”).
**RESEARCH_OPTION:** refuse in long description only.

## P22. MCP / ChatGPT as 2026 checkbox

**Observed:** Polar Core includes Polar MCP; TrueProfit MCP; Lifetimely MCP; Margins MCP.
**Mcfly:** none.
**Pattern:** mid-2026 table stakes for “serious analytics.” Cheap to add *read-only*.

## P23. Multi-store / Amazon add-on as expansion revenue

**Observed:** Lifetimely Amazon $75; Metorik store counts; BeProfit Plus unlimited shops; Polar omnichannel.
**Mcfly:** per-store $39 only.

## P24. Compare-page / vs marketing off-store

**Observed:** TW vs Polar blogs; Polar vs TW page; WeltPixel vs TW $39-flat attack.
**Mcfly site** already has vs suites. **Cannot** put that table on the App Store (testimonials/claims risk).
**Keep vs pages on mcflyads.com.**

## P25. Review-reply theater

**Observed:** TrueProfit replies to **every** visible review, including 1★, within days. Metorik founder-signed. WeTracked replied to 1★. BeProfit replied late to billing 1★.
**Mcfly:** N/A until reviews exist. **Staff this on day one.** Official: replies can save a star edit.
