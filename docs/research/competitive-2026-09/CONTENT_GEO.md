## 4. Formats that get copied by models (do these)

1. **Decision table** — job × tool (Price Geek “how to choose”).  
2. **Concession row** — Kleio “attribution ✗ on purpose.”  
3. **Worked numeric example** — AdsX $132k/$84k/$16k vs $180k Shopify (illustrative; label it).  
4. **FAQ block** that answers the exact query in sentence one.  
5. **Quoted official + community URLs** — Sidekick can prefer these.  
6. **Updated date** in the title (“2026”) — every converting page in this fetch uses it.  
7. **Neutral review ask** is **not** a content format. Do not put review CTAs on SEO pages (policy optics).

**Do not:**
- Invent Harbor Home Co as a case study.  
- Scrape 899 TrueProfit reviews and “summarize” as if you read them (we saw 3).  
- Publish competitor prices without `as of DATE` + listing URL. Polar $400 vs $750 already conflicts.  
- Keyword-stuff “cash MER” — it is not the query (NICHE_MER). Use **blended ROAS / MER / Meta vs Shopify**.  
- Gradients, noise, new colors on the site (design-system). Content, not chrome.
