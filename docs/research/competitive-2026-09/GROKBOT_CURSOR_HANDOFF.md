7. Finance’s real job is **assemble net profit and reconcile the bank**.  
8. Repo religion (no OAuth, ~$79, cash MER) **already disagrees** with live site (no OAuth, $39, Total ROAS) and with MASTER_PLAN Phase 2 (OAuth planned). Flexibility is **inconsistent**, not hypothetical.  
9. Allocation without channel sales assumes sales ∝ spend (`packages/mer-core`). Media buyers will not love that.  
10. There is no public evidence that “anti-attribution” is a **review-winning** phrase. Reviewers praise profit they trust, support with a first name, and tracking that matches Events Manager.

**Brutal line:** priced like TrueProfit, scoped like a spreadsheet, merchandised like Parkour, reviewed like an app that does not exist.
