Security and data posture

An honest page for anyone evaluating an app that reads Shopify sales. Published July 28, 2026.

What we access

  • Shopify Admin API order totals for periods you view (sales for Total ROAS).
  • Ad spend you paste or upload (Meta, Google, other) — not scraped from ad accounts on day one.
  • Settings you enter: contribution margin, optional target Total ROAS.

What we store

  • Shop identity and OAuth session required by Shopify.
  • Spend entries, session/link-click uploads if you use traffic diagnostics, and margin settings.
  • Aggregated MER snapshots for the periods you run — not a customer CRM built from order line items.

Retention

We keep app data while the app is installed. After uninstall or Shopify shop redact webhooks, we delete merchant app data on Shopify’s timelines.

What we do not do

  • We do not install a marketing pixel or run multi-touch attribution.
  • We do not resell merchant data.
  • We do not train public models on your store’s orders or spend.

Subprocessors

Hosting and database providers for the Shopify app (currently planned: Fly.io application hosting and managed Postgres). The marketing site is static on Cloudflare Pages / GitHub Pages. Email for Partner invites is ordinary inbox mail to mcflyadsmmm@gmail.com.

Why “no pixel” is a security feature

Fewer trackers on your storefront means a smaller attack and compliance surface. Total ROAS is computed from Shopify sales and the spend you provide — not from a browser identity graph.
